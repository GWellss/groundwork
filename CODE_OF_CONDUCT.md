# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, by reference. The full text is available at:

https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## Our commitments, in short

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and kind, and give constructive feedback.
- Accept feedback gracefully and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community, not only for the individual.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.

To report a problem, open an issue on this repository, or contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile [@GWellss](https://github.com/GWellss). Reports are handled fairly, and the privacy of the reporter is respected.

Enforcement follows the guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
